# Mission templates, objectives and debrief

A mission definition describes task intent/evaluation, not a script that writes world truth. Define stable ID/version, required capabilities, start conditions, task graph, objectives, constraints, time windows, participants, intelligence assumptions and debrief outputs.

## Mission families
Air tasking may include CAP, intercept, BVR/WVR engagement, escort, strike, CAS, SEAD, reconnaissance, anti-ship, strategic bomber escort/intercept, ferry, refuelling and carrier qualification. Ground tasks may include defend/hold, trench defence, assault/offensive, recon, raid, artillery support, air-defence protection, logistics escort, bridge/fortification action and rescue. Infantry tasks may include village/town clearance, hold/defend, patrol, insertion, engineer works, counterattack, casualty recovery and extraction. Naval tasks may include patrol, sea control, convoy escort/interdiction, blockade, ASW, anti-ship strike, replenishment and amphibious delivery. A task is available only when the platform, payload, crew, location, weather and campaign state support it.

## Objective and debrief model
Progress consumes committed events/reports: destroyed event, occupied-area report, frontline movement, reconnaissance report, rescue event, facility repair, supply delivery, pilot/personnel status, friendly/civilian loss, stores fired/returned, aircraft/vehicle condition and mission time. Distinguish actual objective state from what crew/command knows. Support branching, retask, abort, partial success, friendly fire, civilian harm and post-mission consequences.

Debrief is reconstructed from the event/sortie ledger, not a kill counter alone. Keep confirmed, estimated and reported outcomes separate; include ammunition expenditure, damage, personnel fate, intelligence quality, failures, collateral consequences and campaign effects as applicable. A high mission grade must not erase harm.

Validate unreachable task nodes, missing event source, conflicting conditions, timer edges, unsupported platform/weapon combinations, unexpected unit loss, intelligence uncertainty, debrief reconstruction and repeatable mission seeds.

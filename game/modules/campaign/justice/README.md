# Campaign justice, investigations and accountability

**Owner:** game/modules/campaign/justice. **Status:** architecture only; no justice/investigation runtime exists.

Own legal/civil investigations and accountability records. Tactical systems publish evidence-bearing events; society owns public/community effects; personnel owns individual service/fate; justice evaluates cases under scenario law and institutional policy. A case is not an authoritative rewrite of the underlying event.

## Case lifecycle
Allegation/report → jurisdiction and intake → evidence collection → witness statements and source reliability → investigation → charge/claim or closure → hearing/defence → finding/verdict/settlement → appeal/review → archive. Store allegation, evidentiary basis, confidence, disputed facts, responsible authority, dates and outcome. An accusation is not proof and a public claim is not evidence by repetition.

## Case types
Support civilian harm investigations, unlawful attack/targeting, friendly-fire/fratricide, aircraft or vessel loss involving civilians, treatment/prisoner allegations, property damage, occupation policy, corruption, black-market/criminal activity, contract disputes and civil claims where the campaign's chosen fidelity warrants them. Police, military police, courts, prosecutors, defence counsel, tribunals and oversight bodies have distinct authority/capacity.

## Policy and institutional state
Curfews, emergency powers, wartime censorship, detention, intimidation, corruption enforcement, sanctions and export controls are policy states with scope, authority, duration and review. Case backlogs, evidence access, court capacity, due process and rule-of-law state may change trust and diplomatic relations. Do not turn legal outcomes into simplistic alignment multipliers.

## Validation
Evidence provenance/conflict; allegation versus finding; jurisdiction/authority; procedural transitions; case backlog; independent review; corrections/appeals; public trust and diplomatic consequence; persistence and archive integrity.

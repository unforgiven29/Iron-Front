# Ground mobility physics

Shared models for tracked/wheeled mobility, drivetrain coupling, traction, steering, braking, suspension and terrain interaction. Platform-specific mass, geometry, track/wheel layout, gear ratios and limits remain in each ground-unit module.

Use explicit contact points, surface properties, normal loads, slip, torque and power limits. Movement emerges from forces/resources; do not move a vehicle by directly setting world position. Document fidelity tiers and validity limits.

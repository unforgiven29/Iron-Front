## Seeker and guidance behavior

R-27T is a passive infrared weapon. It does not require radar illumination for terminal homing. Keep separate:
- aircraft-provided cue and its age/uncertainty;
- missile seeker initialization and search state;
- thermal scene, detector response and candidate detection;
- acquisition/track quality and guidance-valid state;
- coast/loss/reacquisition behavior.

The missile must respond to radiance/contrast, target aspect/thermal state, background, atmosphere and decoy observations only to the fidelity supported by the selected seeker profile. Do not transfer R-73's seeker, R-27R's SARH radar, or later seeker improvements into R-27T.

## Vehicle simulation and unknowns

The current evidence set does not establish a usable motor curve, control-surface coefficient database, actuator limits, inertia tensor, warhead fragment model, fuze timing or exact IR seeker transfer function. Treat all as unknown or synthetic-test-only.

Do not set a numeric engagement range from broad published tables as a fixed launch cutoff. Actual trajectory and detection/track outcomes are state-, target-, environment- and equipment-dependent.

## Platform-fit rule

Public military reference sources associate R-27 family weapons with MiG-29 aircraft, while the DCS MiG-29 manual is a simulator reference for specific loadouts. Neither alone proves that every product 9.13 fit or operator software baseline carries R-27T. Make it an explicitly optional research-fit until exact clearance is recorded.

## Required tests

- IR-only seeker; reject SARH/active-radar profiles.
- Passive seeker has no radar illumination dependency.
- Cue/search/detection/acquisition/tracking remain distinct.
- Target/background/thermal scene and decoy change observations over time.
- Candidate ambiguity, false detections, track coast/loss/reacquisition.
- Synthetic-only motor and aero curves; model-tier transparency.
- Safe/arm/fuze/effect separation and deterministic replay.

# GSh-30-1 — MiG-29 product 9.13 gun installation evidence profile

**Stable profile ID:** weapon.gsh-30-1  
**Platform association:** MiG-29 product 9.13 internal cannon.  
**Evidence status:** Government accident-investigation technical report associates MiG-29 family with the GSh-301/GSh-30-1 and lists 150 rounds. The DCS manual states 30 mm, 1,500 rounds/min and 150 rounds, but the manual is simulator reference only. Exact product 9.13 primary maintenance/armament manual remains outstanding.  
**Variant boundary:** Do not confuse GSh-30-1 with the two-barrel GSh-30 family gun or the GSh-6-23 six-barrel cannon.

## Simulation requirements

The GSh-30-1 must be a distinct product definition from the M61A1. Do not reuse its gun mechanism, cyclic rate or installation. The weapon package owns the gun and selected projectile ammunition definitions; MiG-29 platform integration owns the left-nose gun mounting, feed/munition routing, aircraft trigger and indication. Shared projectile ballistics and impact damage remain in their canonical physics owners.

Model actual ammunition count, feed state, trigger/fire authorization, shot timing, recoil/moment, projectile initial condition, dispersion, heat and stoppage only to the level supported by evidence. Exact internal mechanism and detailed firing-cycle timings remain unknown in the current source set.

## Required tests

- 30 mm product identity remains distinct from GSh-30 and GSh-6-23.
- MiG-29 internal installation and 150-round baseline are configuration data, not universal gun defaults.
- 1,500 rounds/min is tagged simulator-reference unless an applicable primary manual is found.
- Ammunition count, feed, cadence, recoil, projectile ballistics and damage are separate.
- Firing cadence uses simulation ticks, not browser frame rate.
- Empty feed, jam, power/control fault and structural damage are distinct states.
- Deterministic replay and test-fixture provenance are retained.

# R-73A Archer — public technical profile

**Stable profile ID:** weapon.r-73a.archer  
**Family:** short-range air-to-air missile  
**Platform association:** MiG-29 product 9.13; publicly associated with MiG-29 family, but exact 9.13 production/operator clearance should be attached to the selected fit.  
**Evidence status:** U.S. Army ODIN government reference plus historical secondary reference and simulator-only loadout evidence.  
**Variant boundary:** R-73A is the domestic Russian-service designation. R-73E is an export variant; R-73M/R-74 developments are later variants. Do not copy R-73E or R-73M/R-74 parameters into R-73A.

## Simulation behavior

R-73A requires a distinct IR seeker profile, not a scalar "IR missile" label. Model:
- launch cue and boresight/helmet-sight handoff only if the aircraft fit supplies a valid cue;
- seeker field of regard and scan/track behavior only from the selected variant data;
- thermal target/background observations and uncertainty;
- seeker acquisition versus track maintenance;
- missile body orientation, control surface state, actuator response and saturation;
- any thrust-vector/gas-dynamic control only where exact R-73A evidence proves its use on the selected product version; do not infer a control architecture from secondary descriptions of other variants;
- flare/decoy and clutter discrimination through a time-varying seeker observation stream, not a hard-coded "flare resistant" rating.

## Platform integration

The MiG-29 cockpit/weapon control system must define target cueing, helmet sight presence/configuration, launcher interface, captive power/coolant requirements if applicable, trigger/consent logic and station compatibility. The missile package must not assume the aircraft always has an operational helmet-mounted sight or grant a fixed off-boresight cue without a functioning compatible system.

## Required tests

- R-73A vs R-73E vs R-73M/R-74 configuration rejection.
- Cue absent/stale/valid and helmet-sight fit enabled/disabled.
- Search, detection, acquisition, track and track loss are distinct.
- Aspect/thermal scene/background/flare/obscuration alter observations; do not use a fixed flare susceptibility.
- Gimbal/rate limits and actuator saturation use sourced profiles or remain explicitly unavailable.
- Motor/aero test fixtures are synthetic-only until product-specific data are found.
- Deterministic replay and safe/arm/fuze/warhead coupling.

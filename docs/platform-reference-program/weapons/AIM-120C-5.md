# AIM-120C-5 AMRAAM — public technical profile

**Stable profile ID:** weapon.aim-120c-5.amraam  
**Family:** medium-range air-to-air guided missile  
**Platform association:** F-16C Block 50 candidate fit. Aircraft software, OFP and station/launcher fit must explicitly select the C-5; broad F-16 compatibility is not proof of every configuration's clearance.  
**Evidence status:** Variant-specific public geometry/mass plus family-level government description of architecture. Detailed flight performance remains classified or unavailable.  
**Variant boundary:** Do not merge AIM-120A/B, C-4, C-5, C-6, C-7 or D into one profile. AIM-120C and D use reduced-span wings relative to A/B; later D-specific navigation/data-link improvements must not be attributed to C-5.

## Functional architecture

The missile profile must represent separate physical/functional sections and state:
- guidance/navigation and inertial reference;
- aircraft/missile umbilical and permitted data link;
- active-radar seeker and terminal acquisition processor;
- target detector/fuze interface;
- armament/warhead section;
- solid rocket propulsion;
- control section and actual actuators.

The launch aircraft supplies a finite-quality cue and initial target state estimate, not a perfect target transform. The missile must propagate its own navigation uncertainty between updates. A data-link update is a timestamped measurement/correction, not a direct replacement of the missile's state with hidden truth.

## Midcourse, handover and terminal states

Implement distinct phases:
1. Captive, power/initialization and BIT.
2. Launcher release request and separation.
3. Inertial navigation initialization and boost.
4. Midcourse navigation and coast/boost flight as motor state dictates.
5. Data-link update received, missed, delayed, stale or rejected.
6. Predicted search/activation condition.
7. Seeker search and candidate detection.
8. Candidate association and track initiation.
9. Terminal guidance, guidance degradation, track loss or reacquisition.
10. Fuze, intercept, miss, dud or self-destruct/expiry.

Do not model "active radar" as instant perfect lock from launch. The missile's terminal seeker has its own scan/search, detection, clutter/environmental limitations, field of regard, revisit timing, track quality and uncertainty. Exact C-5 details for those items are not established by this public profile; implement them as unknown until sources are acquired or as explicitly synthetic test profiles.

## Dynamics and controls

Use a declared model tier (point-mass, 3-DoF or full 6-DoF). Do not call a point-mass model six-degree-of-freedom. The runtime must account for:
- mass and CG evolution during motor burn;
- thrust impulse and burnout, with C-5 thrust-time curve currently unknown;
- body drag/lift/side-force, aerodynamic moments and control-surface effectiveness only to the level supported by data;
- fin actuation rate/position limits, command latency and saturation;
- gravity, atmosphere, wind and target-motion estimates;
- actuator, navigation, seeker, link and power faults.

Do not invent a maximum g limit, exact speed, no-escape zone, launch range, motor impulse or seeker sensitivity. Such values are either unavailable, condition-dependent or classified in the cited sources.

## Aircraft and launcher integration

The F-16 stores/SMS integration defines rail or eject-launcher station, umbilical and pre-launch initialization, exact permitted station/loading combinations, aircraft software compatibility and release envelope. The C-5 product profile must not decide which F-16 block/OFP can carry it.

## Required tests

- C-5 dimensions/mass and rejection of C-7/D-only features.
- Cue uncertainty growth and data-link delivery, delay, drop, corruption and stale-update behavior.
- Midcourse-to-terminal handover and search-volume uncertainty.
- Terminal detection/no-detection, multiple candidates and track swap/loss.
- Active-radar emission observable to the RF environment and other sensors; seeker does not inspect another radar's private tracks.
- Motor burnout/mass change using a synthetic curve until exact data exists.
- Point-mass/3-DoF/6-DoF model-tier declaration and validation.
- Safe/arm, proximity-fuze, miss/dud and warhead interaction.
- Deterministic replay under identical observations and configuration.

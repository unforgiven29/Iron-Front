# 08 — Target Interaction and Radar Cross-Section

## Purpose
Describe how objects scatter incident radar energy and how target properties influence the returned signal.

## Categories
- **Radar cross-section:** effective scattering area, units, frequency dependence, and limits of constant-RCS assumptions.
- **Aspect dependence:** nose, side, tail, beam, and changing aspect during maneuver.
- **Configuration dependence:** stores, control surfaces, doors, landing gear, rotors, and configuration effects where data supports them.
- **Frequency and polarization dependence:** scattering changes with wavelength and polarization.
- **Scattering components:** specular reflection, diffuse scattering, edge effects, and simplified physical contributions.
- **Cavities and inlets:** conceptual treatment of engine inlets, cavities, and internal reflections; do not claim exact results without validated geometry/material data.
- **Target fluctuation:** Swerling-style statistical models and their assumptions.
- **Target motion:** range rate, acceleration, rotation, vibration, and micro-Doppler contributions.
- **Multiple objects:** formations, closely spaced objects, and return separation.
- **Data provenance:** distinguish measured, published, estimated, and synthetic signatures.

## Interactions
Target response combines with transmit power, antenna gain, propagation, receiver noise, and processing. RCS alone must never guarantee detection.
